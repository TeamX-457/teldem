About TELDEM

Building the sensing layer for African homes and businesses.

TELDEM Technologies is a Nigerian smart-monitoring hardware and AI company. We build affordable devices and a shared AI platform that tell people what's happening in the spaces they care about — before it becomes a problem.

Vision

To build an African AI platform that makes real-time monitoring and proactive alerts accessible to everyone — not just those who can afford enterprise IoT.

{% include "partials/icons/act.html" with cls="h-7 w-7 text-teldem-green" %}

Mission

To develop affordable, intelligent solutions that help people monitor, protect and manage their homes, vehicles and businesses — with technology that speaks plainly and acts in time.

Why "TELDEM"?

TELDEM — "Tell Them" — is a promise. Monitoring only matters if it actually reaches the person who needs to know, in language they understand, in time to act. That's the standard every product decision is measured against.

We don't just measure. We tell them.
