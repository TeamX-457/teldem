How it works

Sense. Analyse. Understand. Inform. Act.

Every TELDEM Smart AI Device runs the same five-stage pipeline, whichever category it belongs to. Here's what actually happens between a sensor reading and the message that lands on your phone.

{% include "partials/icons/sense.html" with cls="h-4 w-4" %} STEP 01

Sense

Purpose-built sensors capture real environmental data continuously — temperature, gas level, motion, impact, smoke or door state, depending on the device. This is the raw signal layer.

{% include "partials/icons/analyse.html" with cls="h-4 w-4" %} STEP 02

Analyse

Readings are processed in real time and compared against each device's normal operating pattern, filtering out noise and one-off spikes from genuine signal.

{% include "partials/icons/understand.html" with cls="h-4 w-4" %} STEP 03

Understand

The platform checks the reading against context: is this device armed? Is this metric inside its safe range? Has a similar alert already fired recently? This is what turns a number into a decision.

{% include "partials/icons/inform.html" with cls="h-4 w-4" %} STEP 04

Inform

If a threshold is crossed, you get a clear, plain-language alert — scored by severity — not a raw chart you have to interpret yourself. Critical alerts are escalated immediately.

{% include "partials/icons/act.html" with cls="h-4 w-4" %} STEP 05

Act

Resolve the alert, arm or disarm a device, or escalate to your team — directly from the TELDEM dashboard, without switching tools.
